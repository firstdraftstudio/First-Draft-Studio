Refund & Cancellation Policy

First Draft Studio · Last updated 9 October 2026

If a project needs to stop or a concern arises, requests are reviewed in context. This policy explains how to submit a request and how work completed, approvals and third-party costs may be considered.

1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains how a client may raise a cancellation or refund request for website design and development services from First Draft Studio. The outcome depends on the accepted project proposal, the stage reached, the work performed, any third-party commitments and applicable law. This policy does not establish a blanket non-refundable rule.

2. How to submit a request

Send a request through the Contact & Grievance route or, for an existing account-based project, through your account inquiry conversation at /account/inquiries. Include your name, project or inquiry reference if available, the date of the proposal, and whether you are requesting cancellation, a refund, or a review. Explain the reason and identify any deliverables already received or approved. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.

3. How requests are assessed

  • Before work begins: We will review whether work has started, whether any agreed preparation has been completed, and whether domain, hosting or other third-party costs have already been incurred. Any refund or deduction must be consistent with the accepted agreement and applicable law.
  • During discovery or design: We will consider work completed to date, time spent, materials produced, and any agreed milestones. We will explain the basis of any proposed adjustment rather than applying an undisclosed automatic deduction.
  • During development: We will review the functioning work, components or milestones completed, any preview or deliverable supplied, and any external costs committed for the project.
  • After a deliverable is approved or handed over: We will assess the concern against the approved scope, any defects reported, the project agreement and applicable law. Approval does not waive non-excludable statutory rights or remedies.

4. Domain, hosting and third-party costs

Domain registration, hosting, paid software, stock assets, payment services and other third-party costs may be subject to separate provider terms and may not be recoverable from the provider. If the client is responsible for such costs under the accepted proposal, that will be considered in the review. We will not invent a fixed deduction or claim a cost was incurred without a reasonable basis.

5. Review and response

We will acknowledge requests through the available contact channel and may ask for reasonable clarification or supporting records. The owner must confirm a practical response target and refund-processing timeline before this policy is published. Where a refund is agreed, the method and expected timing will be communicated to the client. Statutory timelines and remedies, where applicable, take precedence.

6. Disagreement or escalation

If you disagree with an outcome, reply to the written response and ask for a review, explaining the specific point you believe has been overlooked. We will review the proposal, communication, delivery record and applicable policy again. This internal process does not remove any rights available under applicable consumer or other law.

7. No unapproved commercial promise

Specific refund percentages, cancellation fees, deadlines, advance-payment treatment and exclusions are not stated here because they have not been approved by the studio owner. These must be decided and recorded in the project proposal before a client accepts the work.
