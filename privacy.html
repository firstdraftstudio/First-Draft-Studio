Privacy Policy

First Draft Studio · Last updated 9 October 2026

Your information should be handled with care. This policy explains what the site may collect, why it is used, which providers support the service, and how to request access, correction or deletion.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how First Draft Studio (“the studio”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information when you visit our website, create an account, submit an inquiry, communicate with us, or work with us on a website project. It is written for an India-based service and should be read alongside the terms agreed for any specific project.

The legal identity and formal contact details of the service operator have not been supplied in the project information available for this draft. Those details must be added before publication. This policy does not claim that compliance with every applicable law has been certified.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account and profile information: name, email address, business name, phone number and other profile details you choose to provide when registering or updating an account.
  • Inquiry and project information: business type, project goals, requirements, selected package, budget or agreed price, requested features, status, timeline, and content you provide.
  • Messages and support records: messages sent through account or inquiry features, replies, and project-related communication.
  • Technical and session information: information necessary for sign-in, session continuity, security and operation of the site. The Supabase authentication client may store session data in browser storage. Hosting, browser and service providers may also process limited technical logs.
  • Information you voluntarily share: files, images, text, business information or other material supplied for a proposed or active project.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or sensitive information unless we specifically establish a secure and necessary process for it.

3. How we use information

We use information to respond to requests, understand project requirements, manage accounts, communicate about inquiries and projects, prepare proposals, deliver agreed services, provide support, protect the website, maintain records, and meet applicable legal obligations. We should not use personal information for an unrelated purpose in a way that is inconsistent with applicable law or the notice given to you.

4. Storage and service providers

The website uses Supabase for its backend and database services, including account authentication and project-related records. Information submitted through features connected to Supabase may be processed on Supabase infrastructure under its service terms and privacy documentation. Website hosting and deployment may involve Cloudflare. We do not control every aspect of these providers' infrastructure, policies or availability.

We may share the minimum information reasonably necessary with service providers that help operate the website or deliver a project. We may also disclose information where required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a genuine business transfer, subject to applicable law. We do not sell personal information.

5. Cookies, browser storage and sessions

The site is designed to use browser storage as needed for Supabase authentication and session management. Authentication/session data may remain in your browser until you sign out, clear browser data, or the session expires or is refreshed. The site may also use essential technical storage needed for functionality. This draft does not assert that analytics or advertising cookies are in use. If additional analytics, advertising or non-essential tracking tools are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated first.

6. Retention

We retain account, inquiry, message and project records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain project history, resolve disputes, keep appropriate business records, protect the website, and comply with legal requirements. Retention periods may differ by record type. When information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or remove identifying details, subject to lawful retention needs, backups and technical limitations.

7. Security and its limits

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including authenticated access and database authorization policies. No website, network transmission, storage system or third-party service can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to information depends in part on correct configuration of Supabase Row Level Security, administrator permissions, account security and deployment settings. Do not share your password, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Your choices and requests

Subject to applicable law and reasonable identity verification, you may request access to personal information associated with you, correction of inaccurate details, deletion where appropriate, or information about how your data is handled. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security or dispute-resolution reasons. You may also sign out and manage browser-stored session data through your browser settings, though clearing local data does not itself delete server-side records.

To make a privacy request, use the contact route described on our Contact & Grievance page and clearly state “Privacy Request”, the email or account identifier associated with the information, and whether you are requesting access, correction or deletion. Do not include a password. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting.

9. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and people able to enter into service arrangements under applicable law. Please do not create an account or submit personal information if you are not legally able to do so without the required consent or authorization.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The updated date above indicates the latest revision of this draft. For questions or requests, use the studio's inquiry route at first-draft-studio website contact section or the Contact & Grievance page. Formal contact details must be confirmed by the owner before publication.
