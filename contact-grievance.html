Contact & Grievance

First Draft Studio · Last updated 9 October 2026

A clear route matters when you have a privacy request, service complaint, cancellation request or other concern.

1. Contact route

First Draft Studio's public website includes an inquiry section at the main website contact section. Use it to describe the issue and the best way to reach you. If you already have an account and an active inquiry or project, you can also sign in and use your account inquiry area to keep the issue connected to the project record.

Important: The project files currently do not provide a verified public email address, phone number, postal address, or registered legal identity. The public inquiry form's current frontend handler still contains an unconfigured email placeholder, so its ability to deliver a message has not been verified. The owner must supply and test a genuine contact channel before this page is published as the formal grievance route.

2. Privacy requests

For access, correction or deletion requests, state “Privacy Request” and describe the record or account involved. Include an email address or account identifier sufficient to help us locate the record, but never send your password. We may request reasonable identity verification before disclosing or changing personal information.

3. Service complaints and delivery concerns

Explain what happened, the project or inquiry reference, relevant dates, and the outcome you are seeking. Include copies of relevant written approvals or proposal details if available. We will review the information and respond through a contact method that has been verified with you.

4. Cancellations and refunds

State whether you are asking to cancel, requesting a refund, or appealing a previous decision. Include the project reference and stage reached. See the Refund and Cancellation Policy for the assessment process.

5. Grievance handling

We intend to review complaints fairly, acknowledge the issue, request relevant information if necessary, and explain the outcome. A named grievance contact, response timeline and escalation contact have not been provided and must be confirmed by the owner before publication. Nothing in this process limits rights available under applicable law.

6. Owner details to publish

  • Full legal name of the person or entity operating First Draft Studio, and its business/brand relationship.
  • A monitored email address for privacy and grievance requests, and optionally a business phone or WhatsApp contact.
  • Postal address and other business details where legally required or voluntarily provided.
  • Name or role of the grievance contact and an owner-approved acknowledgement and resolution target.
